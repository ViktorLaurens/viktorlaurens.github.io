## Why under water

[Pendulum Lab](/projects/pendulum-lab/) ended on an underwater manipulator in the plane. This
project takes the same discipline into three dimensions and into water, where three things
change that no pendulum and no fixed-base arm can ask:

1. **Where is safe, when standing still is not an option?** The current never stops and
   thrust is finite, so the safe state is not standing still but a controller holding
   station. The safe set depends on the current and on how well the current is known.
2. **What does the camera buy?** The structure is in view. A pose relative to it, with an
   error bound that grows with turbidity, can replace a coarse acoustic fix for the one thing
   that matters: not touching the structure while reaching it.
3. **What may be learned inside the physics?** Hydrodynamics dissipate energy and added mass
   is symmetric. A learned model that keeps that structure cannot create energy, and what
   that buys inside an optimizer is measurable.

## One mission, never widened

Every chapter flies the same mission. A BlueROV2 Heavy with a four-joint arm, in a current
with gusts, next to a seabed structure with a keep-out zone, approaches, holds station,
reaches a handle, grasps an object of unknown mass, buoyancy and drag, lifts it clear and
carries it back, without entering the keep-out zone.

## How it is made

The same way as Pendulum Lab: one system, one fixed task, every claim with a figure.
Everything is Python, with MuJoCo for the contact and the camera; every number comes from the
same code, and the results come from simulation.
